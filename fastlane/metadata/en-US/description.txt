A ghost of your menu bar still visible on a dark background? That's image retention — and PixelWash is the fastest way to deal with it.

PixelWash runs four proven wash patterns in fullscreen on every connected display, exercising each pixel so temporary retention fades: RGB noise flickers every pixel through random colors, solid-color cycling drives the full palette edge to edge, moving bars sweep diagonally across the panel, and an inverting checkerboard targets sharp-edged ghosts.

Start a wash the moment you notice a stuck image, let it run for a few minutes — or a few hours for stubborn cases — and stop it with a click or a keypress.

• Four wash modes: RGB noise, solid colors, moving bars, checkerboard
• Auto-mix: rotates through your selected modes at an interval you choose
• Covers every connected display at once, at native resolution
• Tempo control from gentle to aggressive
• Keeps your display awake while a wash is running
• Live preview in the window before you go fullscreen
• No account, no network, no data collection — it draws pixels, that's all

Honest fine print: washing helps with temporary image retention, common on OLED and some LCD panels. Permanent burn-in — pixels physically aged unevenly over years — cannot be fully reversed by any software. The earlier you wash, the better the odds.
